Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 8, 2026

Summary first Welo is built for creators. Your creations belong to you. This Privacy Policy explains what information the Welo team does (and does not) collect, how we use it, and your choices regarding it. We keep it simple.

1. Information We Collect

Content you create

Photos you pick, edits you make, and artwork you save are by default kept on your device only. We do not automatically upload them to our servers.

Device & usage data

We may receive basic technical information (device model, OS version, app version, crash reports, and aggregated feature usage) so we can improve the App.

Purchase information

In-app purchases and coin transactions are processed by Apple. We may receive a receipt or transaction ID to validate purchases and grant coins.

Contact you submit

If you email us, we keep only the message and any contact info you provide, so we can reply to you.

2. How We Use Information

  • To operate and maintain the App — rendering previews, storing local drafts, and delivering in-app purchases.
  • To personalize the app experience (for example, remember your favorite filters on the device).
  • To detect and fix crashes, bugs, and quality issues.
  • To respond to support messages and customer requests.
  • To comply with legal obligations, court orders, or enforceable government requests.

3. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We only share data in these narrow cases:

  • Vendors who help us. Trusted service providers (such as analytics, crash reporting, or payment verification tools) receive only what is necessary to perform their job and are bound by confidentiality obligations.
  • Apple. App Store transactions, receipts, and standard platform reporting are handled through your Apple ID, per Apple's privacy policy.
  • Legal requirements. If we have a good-faith belief that access, use, preservation, or disclosure is reasonably necessary to comply with laws, regulations, legal process, or enforceable governmental requests.
  • Business transfers. In the event of a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, your information may transfer as part of that transaction, subject to standard confidentiality protections.

4. Your Choices & Rights

  • Local control. You can delete edited artworks or drafts at any time from the App's gallery or through the Photos app on your device.
  • Opt out of analytics / tracking. You can disable personalized advertising or app tracking from your device's Settings > Privacy & Tracking.
  • Access, correction, deletion. Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information we hold about you. Email us and we will respond within a reasonable timeframe.
  • California users. If you are a California resident, the CCPA grants you additional rights; contact us to exercise them.
  • EEA / UK users. You have rights under GDPR, including to access, rectify, erase, restrict, object, and port data.

5. Retention

We retain information only for as long as needed for the purposes described in this policy — usually until you delete the content or close your implied relationship with us by deleting the App, plus any period required by law, accounting, or dispute resolution (for example, tax or purchase records are kept as required).

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will promptly delete it.

7. Updates to This Policy

We may revise this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will make that clear within the App. Your continued use of Welo after an update means you accept the revised policy.
